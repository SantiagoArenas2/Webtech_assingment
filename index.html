How it works

1

Publish or search

Hosts publish their properties and available rooms. Seekers search by neighborhood, price, and availability date.

2

Apply

The seeker applies to the room they're interested in with a message. The host reviews, shortlists, and schedules a visit.

3

Visit and move in

After the visit, the host accepts an applicant. The lease is closed and the seeker moves into their new room.
